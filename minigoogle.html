The big idea

So what changed?

The web itself became part of the answer.

⌕ Before PageRank

Search engines could lean heavily on the words on a page. Repeat a popular phrase enough times, and a simple system might mistake repetition for usefulness.

More keywords → Higher position
↗ With PageRank

A link from another page acts like a vote. Not all votes are equal: a link from an important page carries more weight than a link from an obscure one.

A little math, made friendly

The formula behind the votes

Open the formula and hover over any term to see what it means.

What is happening mathematically?
PR(A) = (1 − d) / N + d × Σ(PR(T) / C(T))
Each page gets a small baseline score, plus weighted votes from pages linking to it.
PR(A)PageRank score of page A
dDamping factor: 0.85
NTotal pages: 6
TA page that links to A
C(T)Number of links leaving T
